## Findings (2026-07-21 run, 2016-01-01 – 2020-12-31 — tables above regenerate on re-run)

A third data point alongside `result-qullamaggie-portfolio-v4.md` (2020-2026) and
`result-qullamaggie-portfolio-v4-2010-2015.md` (2010-2015). This window is friendlier to the
strategy than 2010-2015 but tougher than 2020-2026, and it breaks the "s20 wins both tables"
pattern seen in the other two runs.

1. **QQQ is a moderate hurdle, not the near-unbeatable one it was in 2010-2015.** QQQ's MaxDD
   here (-28.56%, Calmar 0.821) reflects only the COVID crash — the deeper 2022 tech bear that
   drags QQQ's Calmar down to 0.563 in the 2020-2026 run falls outside this window. Only 11 of
   40 config/size/RSI cells clear QQQ's Calmar here (best: s20 RSI<70 @3%, 1.514) vs. essentially
   all of them in 2020-2026 and none in 2010-2015 — confirms the benchmark-beating bar moves with
   which crash happens to fall inside the evaluation window, not with the strategy's own quality.
   Against SPY (Calmar 0.388) the bar is easy again: only the two most concentrated/loosest cells
   (s15 RSI<70|>80 @7%: 0.379, s12 RSI<70|>80 @7%: 0.384) land marginally below it.
2. **RSI<70 reasserts itself even more strongly than in 2020-2026** — it wins 19 of 20
   config×size cells here (2020-2026: 16/20; 2010-2015: only 7/20, the outlier). s20 and s17 favor
   RSI<70 at *every* size (e.g. s20 @3%: 1.514 vs 1.200; s17 @3%: 1.394 vs 1.032). Across all three
   periods run so far, RSI<70 is now the majority winner in 2 of 3, and decisively so in this one —
   2010-2015 looks like the anomaly, not the rule.
3. **Position-size sizing matches the 2020-2026 pattern (3-4% sweet spot), not 2010-2015's.**
   Calmar decays monotonically with size for every config here (e.g. s20 RSI<70: 1.514 @3% down to
   0.476 @7%). Skip counts at 3% sizing (s20 RSI<70: 449) sit between 2010-2015 (5) and 2020-2026
   (724) — consistent with a steadily growing pool of $1.5B+-mcap, liquid candidates over time, not
   a one-off regime flip. The more names pass the filters, the more capital gets crowded out at
   large size, and the more 3-4% sizing wins.
4. **s20 is still the Calmar leader (3 of the top-5-by-Calmar slots) but for the first time does
   not also dominate Final$.** The top-5-by-Final$ table here is entirely s17/s15 (best: s17
   RSI<70|>80 @7%, $125,296 — the largest Final$ of any cell across all three periods run so far,
   but with a rough -43.13% MaxDD and only a middling 0.768 Calmar). The "prefer s20, it wins both
   tables" heuristic from the other two runs doesn't fully hold in 2016-2020: s20 remains the
   safer/most consistent pick, but s17/s15 at larger size can out-earn it for those willing to
   trade Calmar for absolute return.
5. **This period's CAGRs are the highest of the three windows for mid-size configs** (many cells
   in the high-20s/low-30s%) — 2016-2020 combines a long 2016-2019 bull run with the sharp
   V-shaped COVID recovery, close to ideal conditions for a momentum breakout strategy: large
   trending gains punctuated by one brief, sharply-reversed drawdown rather than a grinding bear.

## Findings & Ideas to Improve Portfolio Performance

**Shortening the window to 2018-2026 reshuffles the leaderboard — `s17_tr20`@3% is now the best combo (Calmar 1.108), a huge jump from its 0.793 over the full 2010-2026 window.** This is the headline result, and it should be treated with real caution rather than taken as a new "true" optimum. Dropping 2010-2017 disproportionately removes several of `s17_tr20`'s historically mediocre years (2010 +8.7%, 2011 -8.4%, 2014 +12.8%, 2016 +11.7% — all so-so in the full-history run), while keeping its strong recent years intact. That's a textbook illustration of **window-selection risk**: tuning on a shorter, more recent slice can flatter whichever strategy happened to have a weaker distant past and a stronger recent stretch, without that being a genuine improvement in the underlying edge.

**In contrast, `s20_tr20`'s size behavior replicates consistently across both windows — this is the more trustworthy finding.** In the full 2010-2026 sweep, `s20_tr20`'s Calmar peaked at 6% (0.832) and collapsed at 7% (0.695) before a partial rebound at 8%.

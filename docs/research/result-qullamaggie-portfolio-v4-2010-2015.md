## Findings (2026-07-21 run, 2010-01-01 – 2015-12-31 — tables above regenerate on re-run)

This period is a stress test of the 2020-2026 conclusions (`result-qullamaggie-portfolio-v4.md`) against an
earlier, calmer, and much thinner-liquidity regime. Several of that report's takeaways do **not** transfer.

1. **QQQ buy & hold is unbeaten on risk-adjusted terms here.** QQQ's own -16.09% MaxDD makes its Calmar
   (0.983) and Sortino (0.888) higher than every one of the 40 config/size/RSI cells — the best is s20
   RSI<70|>80 @6% (Calmar 0.744). In the 2020-2026 run every cell beat QQQ's Calmar (0.563); here none does.
   SPY (Calmar 0.530) is still clearable — every s20/s17/s15 size does, but **s12 never does** (best 0.461).
   On absolute dollars the strategy still wins in the better cells: s20 RSI<70|>80 @5-7% and a few s17/s15
   cells beat QQQ's $72,292 Final$ (best: s20 RSI<70|>80 @7%, $82,396, +14%).
2. **The RSI filter verdict reverses.** The 2020-2026 run found RSI<70 (excluding 70-80 reentries) beat the
   RSI<70|>80 default in 16/20 cells. Here the default wins 13/20, and s20 flips hardest — RSI<70|>80 wins at
   *every* size (e.g. @6%: 0.744 vs 0.600), where in 2020-2026 RSI<70 won 3 of 5 s20 sizes. The one config
   that stays consistent across both periods is s12, where RSI<70 wins at every size in both runs. RSI-filter
   choice is evidently regime- and threshold-dependent, not a fixed constant.
3. **The position-size sweet spot flips too.** Signal supply is far thinner here — skip counts range 19-456
   vs. 724-2436 for the same configs/sizes in 2020-2026 (fewer $1.5B+-mcap, liquid names existed in the early
   2010s), so cash sits idle even at small sizing (s20 @3% Uninv% 38.0% vs 16.1% in 2020-2026). With scarce
   candidates, bigger positions deploy more capital without the crowding/concentration penalty that hurt
   Calmar at 6-7% in 2020-2026: s20 RSI<70|>80 Calmar *rises* from 0.406 @3% to a peak of 0.744 @6%. The prior
   report's "3-4% is the sweet spot" is a signal-supply artifact of that period, not a universal rule —
   sizing should track available candidate volume, not be fixed.
4. **s20 remains the most robust config across both periods** — it dominates both top-5 tables here just as
   it did in 2020-2026, even though its best RSI variant and best size both changed. That is the one durable
   conclusion: prefer the tightest %-above-SMA50 threshold and re-tune RSI-filter/position-size per regime
   rather than assuming the 2020-2026 defaults transfer as-is.
5. **Idiosyncratic (single-name) drawdown dominates regardless of the macro backdrop.** Strategy MaxDD still
   reaches -24% to -41% here even though the benchmarks were far calmer than 2020-2026 (SPY -19.42%/QQQ
   -16.09% vs. -34.10%/-35.62%). Since most of the strategy's drawdown doesn't come from market beta, it
   can't shrink just because the market did — which is exactly why it fails to clear QQQ's Calmar in a
   period where QQQ itself barely drew down.
